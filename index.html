Grand Piano

A clean-room iPad-first virtual piano inspired by the interaction of MuseScore's browser piano.

Sound: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, Yamaha C5 recordings, licensed CC BY 3.0. Samples are loaded from the public sfzinstruments repository and cached locally by the app.

This version adds full 16-layer velocity mapping, room options, touch curves, preload, and subtle mechanical / resonance enhancement.

This app does not include or copy MuseScore code or proprietary assets.
